For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, contact privacy@stormpine.com. Our data protection contact is [DPO / privacy contact].

2. Scope

This policy covers personal data we process when you use Stormpine as an individual member, visit our website, or connect an integration such as LinkedIn or email. Where we provide the service through an organisation (for example an a-kasse or outplacement partner), a separate agreement may also apply.

3. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account details: name, email, authentication data.
  • The content you create: opportunities, contacts, notes, interactions, tasks, materials such as CVs and bios.

Data you import, with your consent

  • From LinkedIn: your profile, positions, education, skills, and your own connections and activity, via LinkedIn's official data-portability channels or your own data export. See section 6.
  • From email: only the specific conversations you choose to log against a contact or opportunity.

Data we collect automatically

  • Usage and device data needed to run and secure the service.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, as described in section 14.

5. AI processing

Stormpine's AI copilot analyses your own opportunities, contacts, notes and materials to summarise what is moving or stalling, suggest next actions, and draft briefings. It is grounded in your data so that it is useful to your situation.

We do not use your data to train public or third-party AI models, and we do not sell it. The copilot makes suggestions for you to accept or ignore; it does not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you or anyone else. You can object to this profiling (see section 11).

6. LinkedIn and third-party imports

Where you connect LinkedIn, we access only your own LinkedIn data, and only with your explicit consent, through LinkedIn's official data-portability channels or a data export you provide. We do not scrape LinkedIn and we do not collect data about people who have not consented, beyond the contact details already in your own network.

When you import your connections, you share limited personal data about those individuals (such as name, employer and role). We process it to run your personal CRM, on the basis of legitimate interests, applying data minimisation. Those individuals have rights over their data too, including to object or request erasure; requests can be sent to privacy@stormpine.com.

You can disconnect an integration at any time. On disconnection we stop importing, and we delete the imported data on request.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf under a data-processing agreement (see section 8), and where required by law. If Stormpine is ever involved in a merger or acquisition, we will tell you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

9. International transfers

Our aim is to keep your data within the EU/EEA. Where a sub-processor processes data outside the EU/EEA, we rely on an approved transfer mechanism under Chapter V of the GDPR. For transfers to the United States, where the provider is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, we rely on that framework's adequacy decision. For any other provider, or where the framework does not apply, we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses together with a transfer impact assessment and supplementary safeguards where needed. We name the provider, the destination, and the mechanism in the sub-processor list.

You can delete your account at any time, after which we delete or anonymise your data within [retention window], except where law requires us to keep certain records.

11. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing. You can also object to profiling by the AI copilot.

To exercise any right, contact privacy@stormpine.com. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In Denmark that is Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).

12. International users and your regional rights

Stormpine is established in Denmark, so we apply European (GDPR) standards of protection to everyone who uses the service, wherever you live. On top of that baseline, some regions give you specific rights or contact points.

European Economic Area

You have the GDPR rights in section 11, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. In Denmark that is Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).

United Kingdom

If you are in the UK, the UK GDPR applies to your data. You have equivalent rights, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), and our UK representative is [UK representative, to be appointed].

California and other US states

If you are a US resident, applicable state privacy laws may give you rights to know, access, correct, delete, and port your personal information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising them. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise a right, contact privacy@stormpine.com.

Switzerland and elsewhere

If you are in Switzerland, the Swiss FADP applies and you may contact the FDPIC. Wherever else you are, we apply the same GDPR-grade protections and honour the core rights described here.

13. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls on a least-privilege basis, and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat sensitive career data with corresponding care, and we will describe formal certifications here only once they are in place.

14. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to run the service, and, with your consent, limited analytics cookies to understand and improve it. You can manage your choices at any time. Full detail is in our cookie policy.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product and our providers change. We will post the new version here with an updated effective date, and for material changes we will give you notice.
