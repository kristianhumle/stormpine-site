Our principles

Six commitments we design around.

Not a compliance checkbox. These shape the product itself, because the audience we build for would notice if they did not.

🗝️

You own your data

Your record is yours. Export it or delete it at any time. We never sell it, rent it, or use it to target ads. You are the customer, not the product.

🕶️

Private by design

A career move is confidential until you choose otherwise. No public profile, no social feed, no broadcasting. Access inside Stormpine follows least-privilege.

⚖️

Built for GDPR

European data protection is the baseline, not an afterthought. Clear lawful basis, real data-subject rights, and the right to complain to your supervisory authority.

🇪🇺

Kept in the EU

Our commitment is to store and process your data within the EU/EEA. Where a service provider sits outside it, we use approved safeguards and tell you who they are.

🔒

Secured, sensibly

Encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular backups. Formal certifications will come as we grow, and we will not claim them before they are real.

🔗

You control connections

Any integration (LinkedIn, email) runs on your explicit consent and only pulls what you authorise. Connect or disconnect whenever you like. We never scrape other people.

Your data, your rights

What you can ask us to do, any time.

Under GDPR these are your rights over your own data. In Stormpine they are meant to be a setting, not a legal battle.

↧

Access & export

Get a copy of everything we hold about you, in a portable format.

✎

Rectify

Correct anything that is wrong or out of date.

⌫

Erase

Delete your account and its data, including anything imported from LinkedIn.

⏸

Restrict & object

Pause certain processing, or object to it, including profiling by the AI copilot.

↺

Withdraw consent

Turn off an integration or withdraw consent at any time, without losing the rest.

§

Complain

Raise a concern with your data protection authority (in Denmark, Datatilsynet).

To exercise any of these, write to privacy@stormpine.com. Full detail lives in the privacy policy.

LinkedIn & integrations

Your data comes in because you bring it, not because we take it.

Where Stormpine connects to LinkedIn or your inbox, it is always through official, consent-based access to your own data. You authorise the connection, you see what is pulled, and you can revoke it in a click.

  • ✓ LinkedIn import runs on your consent through LinkedIn's own data-portability channels, not scraping.
  • ✓ We only ingest the categories needed for a feature you use, and nothing about people who have not consented beyond your own contacts.
  • ✓ Disconnect at any time and the imported data stops flowing, and is deleted on request.
  • ✓ Email capture is scoped to conversations you choose to log, never a blanket read of your inbox.
AI & your data

The copilot works from your material, and keeps it yours.

◎

Grounded, not generic

The AI reasons over your own opportunities, contacts and notes to suggest next moves and prepare briefings. Its value is that it knows your situation.

⛨

Not training fodder

Your data is used to serve you, not to train public models. We do not sell it or feed it into someone else's product.

☝

You stay in control

Suggestions are yours to accept or ignore. Nothing is sent to anyone, and no decision that matters is made about you automatically.
