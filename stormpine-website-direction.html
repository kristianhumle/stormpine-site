Marketing site · v0 direction

One system, from the homepage
into the product.

The Stormpine website inherits the live app's design language 1:1 — same near‑black ground, same periwinkle accent, same Inter, same restraint — so a prospect who clicks “Sign in” feels no seam. Tone is understated and discreet; the product is the proof.

Dark‑first, light variant Hybrid hero (brand + real UI) Understated & discreet Tokens sampled from app · 2026‑07‑29

Guiding principle. The site doesn't invent a brand — it extends the one the product already has. Tokens and tone are locked to the app; the website may lead polish by a half‑step (a cleaner screenshot, a more refined empty state) that the product then catches up to.

07

Guardrails

The site's hardest job is to not look like the category it's positioned against.

✓ Do

  • ✓ Lead with real product UI in the app's dark palette.
  • ✓ Keep the indigo accent the single bold note; everything else quiet.
  • ✓ Write understated, discreet copy — calm control, preparedness.
  • ✓ Present AI modestly, as a supporting proof point.
  • ✓ Use generous whitespace and hairline dividers, like the app.

✕ Don't

  • ✕ Job‑tracker / resume‑builder framing or visuals.
  • ✕ Gamification, streaks, hustle‑culture energy.
  • ✕ Stocky “happy office” photography or busy gradients.
  • ✕ Over‑claim AI (no auto‑briefings / influence maps yet).
  • ✕ Consumer‑freemium loudness or emoji‑as‑headline.
