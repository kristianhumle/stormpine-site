Concept H

Living pursuits, different tempos — evolves G

Context in (product icons); pursuits orbit at their own pace and surface their activity as they move; clarity out.
S Stormpine ProductPricing

Manage your next career move with the context and clarity it deserves.

A private system for experienced leaders managing director, board and advisory pursuits. Keep the relationships, conversations, materials and next steps in one place, with grounded AI that assists you at every stage.

The idea

In → out, alive with activity. Context flows in (left, with the product's own icons); each pursuit orbits at its own pace and surfaces its real activity as it goes; executive clarity flows out (right).

The motion

Three orbits, desynced (~9 / 11 / 13s) so they never line up. As a dot passes a point, that activity — with its icon — pops up, then fades.

Best when

The fullest telling: what feeds in, the living pursuits of different lengths, and the judgement out — product icons make the activity quick to read.
