1. About this policy

This policy explains how Stormpine, operated by [legal entity name], uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and in the product, and how you control them. It sits alongside our privacy policy.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site stores on your device. Similar technologies (local storage, pixels) work the same way. They let a service remember your actions and preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the product is used.

3. How we use them

We keep our use of cookies deliberately narrow. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell data collected through cookies.

Strictly necessary

Required to run the service: sign-in and session, security, and remembering your cookie choices. These do not need consent because the service cannot work without them.

Functional

Remember preferences such as your light or dark theme and language. Set only where relevant to how you use the product.

Analytics

Privacy-respecting measurement of how the product is used, so we can improve it. These are set only with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Consent and control

When required, we ask for your consent to non-essential cookies through a banner, and you can change your choices at any time via [cookie settings link]. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though strictly necessary cookies are needed for the service to function.

6. Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by providers who help us run the service, such as our analytics provider. Where they are non-essential, they are set only with your consent. Each such provider is listed in the table above and covered by a data-processing agreement.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our use of cookies changes. We will post the new version here with an updated effective date.
