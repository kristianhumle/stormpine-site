The data boundary

Integrations that read your world, never other people's.

Stormpine is built from the individual's side of the table. Connections exist to keep your own record current, not to surveil a market or enrich a stranger. That line is deliberate, and it is what makes the whole thing trustworthy.

  • ✓ You connect each integration yourself, and can disconnect it whenever you like.
  • ✓ Stormpine only ever touches your own data, never other people's profiles or inboxes.
  • ✓ Email is matched by metadata; the contents of your messages are never read.
  • ✓ No scraping, no monitoring of third parties, no data brokers in the loop.
  • ✓ GDPR-first and Nordic by design, your record stays private to you.

Coming soon · LinkedIn

Your LinkedIn, brought in by you. Not scraped from it.

LinkedIn is where recruiters, headhunters and board networks live, so it is the obvious thing to connect. The compliant, on-brand way to do it is to let you port your own data in, not to mine the network for everyone else's.

What it will do

  • ✓ Seed your profile on day one with your existing data.
  • ✓ Import your first-degree connections as contacts you already know.
  • ✓ Draft a baseline CV and bio from your real history, ready to tailor per role.
  • ✓ Keep your own posts and activity as private signals for your copilot.

What it will never do

  • ✕ Look up or enrich other people from a LinkedIn URL.
  • ✕ Monitor anyone else's job changes, posts or moves.
  • ✕ Scrape the network or buy scraped data from a broker.
  • ✕ Put your own LinkedIn account at any risk to do it.
🇪🇺 Why a Nordic launch has the edge. EU data-portability rules give members a real, sanctioned route to move their own LinkedIn data into an app they choose. Stormpine is built to use exactly that route.
