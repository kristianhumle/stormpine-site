The data boundary

Your members' privacy is the whole point, not a footnote.

Stormpine is built from the individual's side of the table. You offer access to a private career system, you do not get a window into it. That boundary is what makes leaders trust it, and what makes it a benefit worth offering.

  • ✓ The member owns their record. It stays private to them.
  • ✓ You provide access as a benefit; you do not see a member's private career data.
  • ✓ Any insight shared with you is aggregate and consented, never individual.
  • ✓ GDPR-first, with Nordic data expectations respected by design.

Questions

What partners usually ask.

Do we see our members' data?
No. Stormpine belongs to the individual. You offer access as a benefit, but you do not get a view into a member's private career record. Anything you see is aggregate and consented, for example how many members are active, never one person's opportunities or notes.
How is it priced for organisations?
Partnership pricing is separate from individual plans and depends on your model, whether it is a member benefit, an outplacement programme, or an employer package. We work it out in the conversation rather than list a number here.
Is it GDPR-compliant and Nordic-appropriate?
Yes. Stormpine is a Denmark-based service built GDPR-first, with Nordic data expectations respected by design. Our Trust & privacy approach explains the ownership and data boundary in detail.
Can it be co-branded?
Co-branding and how the benefit is presented to your members are part of what we shape together in a partnership. Tell us how you would like it to appear.
How much effort is it to roll out?
Light. Because members own their own accounts, there is no heavy integration or data migration on your side. We agree how access is offered and how it is introduced to the people you support.
Which organisations is this for?
Professional membership organisations, outplacement providers, career coaching firms, and employers offering transition packages or supporting internal mobility. If you support experienced leaders through career moves, it likely fits.
