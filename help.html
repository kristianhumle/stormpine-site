Frequently asked

The questions people ask first

What is Stormpine, and how is it different from a job tracker?

Stormpine is a Personal Career CRM: a private system for the relationships, conversations, signals and next steps behind your most consequential career moves. A job tracker is built around applications you fire off. Stormpine is built around relationships and long, multi-stage processes, employment, board seats and advisory work alike, and it stays useful between searches, not only during one. Guide: is Stormpine right for me? →

Who can see my data?

You, and only you, unless you deliberately choose to share something. Stormpine is private by design: there is no public profile, no feed, and no discoverability. Your record is not visible to recruiters, employers or other members. If your organisation offers Stormpine as a benefit, your workspace still stays yours, they never see your pursuits or notes. Read Trust & privacy →

Can I import my contacts and network?

Yes. You can add people by hand, bring in a contacts file, or connect LinkedIn and email so the relationships you already have are there from day one. Connections are made on your explicit consent, one source at a time, and you can disconnect any of them whenever you like. Guide: importing your network →

How does the AI copilot work?

The copilot reasons over your own record, not generic career advice. It reads what is in your account to show what is moving versus stalling, flag information that is missing, point to who holds influence in a process, suggest a sensible next step, and prepare a grounded briefing before a conversation. Every suggestion is tied to something already in your account, so you can see why it was made. Guide: working with the copilot →

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Your career record is not used to train models, and it is not shared with other members. The copilot uses your data only to help you, inside your own account. This is a deliberate line: the whole point of a private career system is that it stays private. More on how your data is handled →

Does it handle board seats and advisory roles, not just jobs?

Yes, and that is central to the design. An opportunity in Stormpine can be an employment move, a board seat or an advisory assignment, each with its own timeline and stages. Board and advisory pursuits run for months and are almost entirely relationship-led, so they are treated as first-class, not bolted onto a jobs tool. Guide: board & advisory pursuits →

Can I export or delete everything?

Yes. Your record is yours: you can export your data, and you can delete your account and its contents. We build Stormpine with European data residency in mind, in keeping with its Nordic-first footing and GDPR. If you ever need a hand with an export or a deletion, the team will help. Read the privacy policy →

What does the beta include, and what does it cost?

Stormpine is free while in beta, and no card is required to start. You get the full working product, and your feedback genuinely shapes what we build next. When paid plans arrive, we will tell you well in advance, nothing will start charging by surprise. See plans & pricing →

Is Stormpine available in Danish?

The product is in English today, with Danish coming. Stormpine is Nordic-native by design, built around the way recruiters, a-kasser and board searches actually work here, so a Danish experience is on the near roadmap rather than a distant maybe.

How do I cancel, and what happens to my record?

You can leave at any time from your account settings. Because Stormpine is meant to be durable, a record you keep across a whole career, you can export everything first, and there is no penalty for stepping away and coming back for your next move.
