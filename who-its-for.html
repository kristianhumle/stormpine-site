Board & advisory

The slowest, most relationship-led search you run.

A board seat rarely comes from an application. It comes from a conversation two years ago, a warm introduction, a reputation quietly maintained. These pursuits run for months, sometimes across several boards at once, and the thread is easy to lose between the moments that matter.

  • ✓ Track board and advisory pursuits on their own long timeline, apart from employment
  • ✓ Keep every sponsor, chair and introducer as a living relationship, not a stale contact
  • ✓ Remember the context of each conversation, so a six-month gap never reads as a cold start
  • ✓ See where a nomination is genuinely progressing versus politely idling

Quietly open

Not looking. Not closed either.

You are content where you are, but the right approach would change that. So you keep the door open: a coffee with a headhunter, a note after a good conversation, a relationship worth not letting cool. The trouble is that "quietly open" has no system, so it lives entirely in your head.

  • ✓ Keep recruiter and network relationships warm without a live search running
  • ✓ Note what was discussed and when, so you can pick a thread back up naturally
  • ✓ Capture signals, a role that opened, a mention, a move, as they happen
  • ✓ Be ready to move deliberately the day the right approach lands

Recently placed

You just landed. Do not throw the map away.

The search is over and the offer is signed. Most people close the spreadsheet and lose everything they learned: the relationships, the compensation benchmarks, the reasons this role won. The next move is three or five years out, and it will start from cold unless you keep the record.

  • ✓ Preserve the relationships and context you built, ready for next time
  • ✓ Keep a private note on why this move won, and what you would weigh differently
  • ✓ Stay lightly in touch with the recruiters and sponsors who backed you
  • ✓ Turn a one-off search into a durable career record you own for good

Where Stormpine fits, and where it doesn't

A focused instrument for a few high-stakes moves, not a volume machine.

Being clear about what this is, and isn't, is part of the point.

✓ Built for

  • ✓ A handful of high-stakes moves: a director role, a board seat, an advisory brief
  • ✓ The leader running their own search, from their side of the table
  • ✓ Long, relationship-led pursuits that unfold over months
  • ✓ A private, durable career record you own and keep between moves

✕ Not built for

  • ✕ High-volume applying: hundreds of roles, autofilled and fired off
  • ✕ Entry-level or early-career job hunting
  • ✕ Recruiters managing candidates and their pipeline
  • ✕ Anyone who wants a public profile: Stormpine is private by design

If that is what you need, an application tracker will serve you better. Stormpine is for the handful of moves that actually shape a career.
