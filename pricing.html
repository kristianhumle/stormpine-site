Questions

Fair questions about a free beta.

Is it really free right now?
Yes. While Stormpine is in beta there is no charge and no card required. We would rather have you using it and telling us what works than have your payment details on file.
What will it cost when you start charging?
We have not fixed the numbers. The plan is a free tier plus one premium subscription, priced as a serious professional tool rather than a cheap utility. We will publish real pricing on this page before anything changes, and members who joined in beta keep a founding rate.
Will I be charged automatically when beta ends?
No. There is no card on file, so nothing can be charged without you actively choosing a paid plan. You will get at least 30 days' notice before paid plans begin, and you can simply stay on the Free tier if that suits you.
What happens to my data if I do not upgrade?
It stays with you. Your relationships, notes and history remain in your account on the Free tier. Stormpine is built to be kept between searches, not cancelled after one, so we do not lock your record behind a paywall.
Is my information private?
Career moves are sensitive, and we treat them that way. No ads, and your data is never sold. You are the customer here, not the product. More detail lives on our Trust & privacy page.
Can I cancel?
There is nothing to cancel during beta, and no subscription to unwind. When paid plans exist you will be able to cancel at any time and drop back to the Free tier without losing your history.
