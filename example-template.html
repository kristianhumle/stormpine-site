Leadership comp benchmark worksheet.

Senior packages are rarely won or lost on base salary. This worksheet lays every offer out on the same terms, so you can compare what actually matters, side by side.

WorksheetUpdated Aug 2026Copy per offer

A headline base is the easiest number to compare and the least useful. At the executive level, the difference between two offers usually sits in the variable pay, the equity terms, the notice and severance, and the things that never appear on the first page. Fill in a copy of this worksheet for each offer, and the real comparison becomes obvious.

How to use it

Duplicate the blocks below for each opportunity and complete them as the numbers firm up. Leave a field blank if it is still open, that gap is itself a negotiation item worth raising early.

The best offer on base is often not the best offer. Put them on the same page and the real one tends to declare itself.
