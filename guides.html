Flagship playbook

Running three searches at once without dropping a thread.

The hardest part of a senior move is rarely any single conversation. It is holding four or five of them at different stages, across different firms, without letting the promising one quietly stall while you chase the loud one. This is the method: how to keep every process visible, know what each one is waiting on, and walk into every call with the whole thread in your head.

  • ✓ How to tell a stall from a soft no, and what to do about each
  • ✓ The five-minute pre-call brief that replaces re-reading a thread
  • ✓ Keeping momentum without ever looking anxious to a search consultant
